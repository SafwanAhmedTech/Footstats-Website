About

Hello, my name is Safwan Ahmed, the creator of Footstats. I am a passionate football fan and a 16 year-old kid (at the time of making this website) with a keen interest in programming. This website is my personal project to combine my love for football with my skills in programming and data visualization.

Welcome to Footstats, a football statistics platform built by a fan who believes football is more than just goals and trophies — it's about the numbers, stories, and moments behind every match.

My interest in football statistics started from a curiosity about what happens beyond the scoreline. I wanted to understand player performances, team trends, and the small details that can decide a match.

This website was created to make football statistics easier to explore. Whether you are analysing players, comparing teams, tracking performances, or simply discovering interesting football facts, the goal is to provide clear and useful data for every fan.

On this website, you can find:

Comprehensive team and player statistics

Match results and fixtures

League tables and standings

Insights and analysis of football performances
